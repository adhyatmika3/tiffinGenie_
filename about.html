About TiffinGenie

Helping parents plan healthy and happy school tiffins for their children.

Our Story

TiffinGenie was born from the simple realization that repetitive meals lead to "boring tiffins" and skipped nutrition. Our team came together to build an intelligent engine that understands dietary needs, respects allergies, and ensures variety without any manual effort. We are committed to making healthy eating fun and accessible for every child.

Collaboration

We collaborate with pediatric nutritionists and school health experts to ensure that every recommendation is backed by science. Our goal is to bridge the gap between busy schedules and nutritious meals through technology and expert insights.
