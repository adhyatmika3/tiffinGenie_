Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about TiffinGenie and how it helps parents plan healthy school lunches.

What is TiffinGenie? ▼

TiffinGenie is a personalized nutrition planning platform designed to help parents prepare healthy school tiffins for their children using real-world recipes and smart AI insights.

How does personalization work? ▼

When you start, you provide details like your child's age, dietary preferences (Veg/Non-Veg), and allergies. Our AI engine then curates a weekly plan that balances variety with nutrition.

Can I swap meals if I don't like a suggestion? ▼

Yes! Every meal in your plan can be "swapped" for a similar alternative. The Genie also learns from your choices to improve future suggestions.

Is the data safe? ▼

Absolutely. Your child's profile and your meal preferences are stored securely. We do not share your personal data with third parties.

Is there a mobile app? ▼

TiffinGenie is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can "Add to Home Screen" from your mobile browser and it will function just like an app!
