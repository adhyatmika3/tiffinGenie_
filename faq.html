Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about TiffinGenie and how it helps parents plan healthy school lunches.

What is TiffinGenie? +

TiffinGenie is a personalized nutrition planning platform designed to help parents prepare healthy school tiffins for their children.

How does personalization work? +

Parents add child details such as age, height, weight, food preferences, and allergies. The system then generates suitable meal plans.

Is the nutrition information reliable? +

The platform is designed with guidance from pediatric dietitians to ensure accurate and healthy meal recommendations.

Can I customize the meal suggestions? +

Yes. Parents can modify meal suggestions and menus based on their child’s taste preferences or dietary needs.

Is my child’s data safe? +

Yes. User information is securely stored and used only for personalization and improving the nutrition planning experience.
